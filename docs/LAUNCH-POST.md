## Caption (English)

Evidence database for pet owners affected by the San Jose del Monte animal remains discovery.

Owners upload evidence files from a phone. A case can be printed or exported as a ZIP of the original files. This replaces collecting evidence on USB drives.

Site: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app
Instructions: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app/guide

What the site does
- Stores receipts, messages, photos, certificates and veterinary records
- Builds a timeline and an evidence index for each case
- Exports a case as a print-ready package or a ZIP of the original files
- Accepts reports of earlier incidents
- Accepts information without an account, anonymously if chosen

File handling
- Files are private. They are visible only to the uploader and authorized reviewers
- Originals are not changed. A SHA-256 hash is recorded for each file so a change can be detected
- Contact details are not published
- Limits: 50 MB per file, 300 MB per account

Notices
- The recovered-animals registry has no records. Search returns no results until records are added
- A potential match is an investigative lead, not a confirmed identification
- This platform makes no determination of legal responsibility
- Submit only genuine information. Do not alter or fabricate evidence

Requests
1. Administration. This platform was built independently and is not run by any organization. Requesting Luna and Furry Siblings, Animal Kingdom Foundation and the Philippine Animal Welfare Society (PAWS) to take over administration. Representatives: message this page or comment on this post. Access is given only after confirmation through each organization's official channel.
2. Registry records. Organizations or volunteers with authorized access to source documentation of the recovered animals can be set up as reviewers to enter records. Entries can be text only: label name, weight, colors, markings, source. Photos are optional and hidden behind a reveal button. Message this page.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any organization.

## Message to the organizations (send privately, not in the comments)

Subject: Request to take over administration of an evidence platform for the San Jose del Monte case

Hello,

I built an independent web platform for affected pet owners. It replaces collecting evidence on USB drives: owners upload receipts, messages, photos and certificates from their phones, and a case can be printed or downloaded as a ZIP of the original files with a hash manifest. It is live at https://csjdm-29.vercel.app and is not affiliated with any organization.

It also holds a registry for documented recovered animals, with potential matches against owner reports. The registry has no records.

I am requesting that your organization take over its administration. Files are stored unmodified with SHA-256 hashes, every reviewer access is logged, and potential matches are leads only.

Taking over involves
- Two named administrators from your organization
- Ownership of the code repository, the database and file storage (Supabase), and the website hosting (Vercel)
- Responsibility for the personal data collected, including a data protection officer and a privacy notice
- About one hour of setup with me. The steps are in the handover guide

Registry records
- If your organization holds authorized source documentation of the recovered animals, your reviewers can enter records. Entries can be text only. Photos are optional and hidden behind a reveal button.

What I will do
- Transfer the three accounts, rotate every secret key, and remove my access
- Walk your administrators through the reviewer guide and the user management page

What I need from you
- The names of two administrators and an official email address for each
- Confirmation from an officer of the organization through your official page or contact number
- A decision on who pays for the hosting plan

Do not send passwords or keys by message. Access is granted only through service invitations.

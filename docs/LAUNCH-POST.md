## Caption (English)

Evidence scattered across phones, chats and USB drives? Keep it in one place.

Free online evidence database for pet owners affected by the San Jose del Monte animal remains discovery. Upload from your phone. Print it or download everything as a ZIP when authorities ask. No USB drive needed.

What you can do
- Upload receipts, messages, photos, certificates and veterinary records
- Keep everything in one private place with a timeline and an evidence index
- Print your case package or download all your files as a ZIP
- Report an earlier incident
- Submit information anonymously, no account needed

How your files are handled
- Private by default. Only you and authorized reviewers can see them
- Originals are never changed. Each file gets a SHA-256 hash so any change can be detected
- Contact details are never published
- Limits: 50 MB per file, 300 MB per account

Please read
- The recovered-animals registry has no records yet, so searches show no results
- A potential match is an investigative lead only, not a confirmed identification
- This platform makes no determination of legal responsibility
- Submit only genuine information. Do not alter or fabricate evidence

Link: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app (guide: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app/guide)

Help needed
1. Administration: this platform was built independently and is not run by any organization. I am requesting Luna and Furry Siblings, Animal Kingdom Foundation and the Philippine Animal Welfare Society (PAWS) to take over its administration. Representatives: please message this page or comment on this post. Access will be given only after confirming through each organization's official channel.
2. Registry records: organizations or volunteers with authorized access to source documentation of the recovered animals can be set up as reviewers to enter records. Entries can be text only (label name, weight, colors, markings, source). Photos are optional and stay hidden behind a reveal button. Please message this page.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any organization.

## Caption (Taglish)

Nakakalat ba ang evidence sa phone, chat at USB drive? Ipunin sa isang lugar.

Libreng online evidence database para sa mga pet owner na apektado ng natuklasang animal remains sa San Jose del Monte. Mag-upload gamit ang phone. I-print o i-download lahat bilang ZIP kapag humingi ang mga awtoridad. Hindi na kailangan ng USB drive.

Pwede ninyong gawin
- Mag-upload ng resibo, messages, litrato, certificate at veterinary records
- Ayusin ang lahat sa isang pribadong lugar, may timeline at evidence index
- Mag-print ng case package o mag-download ng lahat ng files bilang ZIP

Paano hinahawakan ang files
- Pribado. Kayo lang at ang mga awtorisadong reviewer ang makakakita
- Hindi binabago ang original. May SHA-256 hash ang bawat file para malaman kung may nabago
- Hindi kailanman ipinapakita sa publiko ang contact details
- Limit: 50 MB bawat file, 300 MB bawat account

- Wala pang laman ang registry ng recovered animals, kaya walang lalabas sa search
- Ang "potential match" ay lead lang para sa imbestigasyon at hindi kumpirmadong pagkakakilanlan
- Walang hatol ng legal na pananagutan ang platform na ito
- Magsumite lamang ng totoong impormasyon. Huwag baguhin o gawa-gawain ang evidence

Link: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app (guide: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app/guide)

## Message to the organizations (send privately, not in the comments)

Subject: Request to take over administration of an evidence platform for the San Jose del Monte case

Hello,

I built an independent web platform for affected pet owners. It replaces collecting evidence on USB drives: owners upload receipts, messages, photos and certificates from their phones, and can print their case or download everything as a ZIP with a hash manifest. It is live at https://csjdm-29.vercel.app and is not affiliated with any organization.

It also has a registry for documented recovered animals, with potential matches against owner reports. The registry has no records yet.

I am requesting that your organization take over its administration. Evidence handling is built in: files are stored unmodified with SHA-256 hashes, every reviewer access is logged, and potential matches are leads only.

What taking over involves
- Two named administrators from your organization
- Ownership of the code repository, the database and file storage (Supabase), and the website hosting (Vercel)
- Responsibility for the personal data collected, including a data protection officer and a privacy notice
- Roughly one hour of setup with me. The steps are in the handover guide

Registry records
- If your organization holds authorized source documentation of the recovered animals, your reviewers can enter records. Entries can be text only. Photos are optional and hidden behind a reveal button.

What I will do
- Transfer the three accounts, rotate every secret key, and remove my access
- Walk your administrators through the reviewer guide and the user management page

What I need from you
- The names of two administrators and an official email address for each
- Confirmation from an officer of the organization through your official page or contact number
- Your decision on who pays for the hosting plan

Please do not send any passwords or keys by message. Access is granted only through service invitations.

Thank you.

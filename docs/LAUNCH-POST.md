## Caption (English)

Free evidence database. No USB drive needed.

For pet owners affected by the San Jose del Monte animal remains discovery: upload and keep your evidence here.

What you can do
- Upload receipts, messages, photos, certificates and veterinary records from your phone
- Keep everything in one private place with a timeline and an evidence index
- Print your case package or download all your files as a ZIP when authorities ask
- Report an earlier incident
- Submit information anonymously, no account needed

How your files are handled
- Private by default. Only you and authorized reviewers can see them
- Originals are never changed. Each file gets a SHA-256 hash so any change can be detected
- Contact details are never published
- Limits: 50 MB per file, 300 MB per account

Please read
- The recovered-animals registry is still being compiled, so searches may show no results for now
- A potential match is an investigative lead only, not a confirmed identification
- Some records may contain images of deceased animals. They stay hidden until you choose to reveal them
- This platform makes no determination of legal responsibility
- Submit only genuine information. Do not alter or fabricate evidence

Link: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app

Request to organizations
This platform was built independently and is not run by any organization. I am requesting Luna and Furry Siblings, Animal Kingdom Foundation and the Philippine Animal Welfare Society (PAWS) to take over its administration. Representatives: please message this page or comment on this post. Access will be given only after confirming through each organization's official channel.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any organization.

## Caption (Taglish)

Libreng evidence database. Hindi na kailangan ng USB drive.

Para sa mga pet owner na apektado ng natuklasang animal remains sa San Jose del Monte: dito i-upload at itago ang inyong evidence.

Pwede ninyong gawin
- Mag-upload ng resibo, messages, litrato, certificate at veterinary records gamit ang phone
- Ayusin ang lahat sa isang pribadong lugar, may timeline at evidence index
- Mag-print ng case package o mag-download ng lahat ng files (ZIP) kapag humingi ang mga awtoridad
- Mag-report ng naunang insidente
- Magsumite ng impormasyon nang anonymous, hindi kailangan ng account

Paano hinahawakan ang files
- Pribado. Kayo lang at ang mga awtorisadong reviewer ang makakakita
- Hindi binabago ang original. May SHA-256 hash ang bawat file para malaman kung may nabago
- Hindi kailanman ipinapakita sa publiko ang contact details
- Limit: 50 MB bawat file, 300 MB bawat account

Paalala
- Binubuo pa ang registry ng recovered animals, kaya maaaring walang lumabas sa search sa ngayon
- Ang "potential match" ay lead lang para sa imbestigasyon at hindi kumpirmadong pagkakakilanlan
- May mga record na maaaring may larawan ng namatay na hayop. Nakatago ito hanggang piliin ninyong ipakita
- Walang hatol ng legal na pananagutan ang platform na ito
- Magsumite lamang ng totoong impormasyon. Huwag baguhin o gawa-gawain ang evidence

Link: https://csjdm-29.vercel.app

## Message to the organizations (send privately, not in the comments)

Subject: Request to take over administration of an evidence platform for the San Jose del Monte case

Hello,

I built an independent web platform where affected pet owners can upload and preserve evidence (receipts, messages, photos, certificates), report incidents, and where documented recovered animals can be compared with owner-submitted details. It is live at https://csjdm-29.vercel.app. It is not affiliated with any organization.

I am requesting that your organization take over its administration. Evidence handling is built in: files are stored unmodified with SHA-256 hashes, every reviewer access is logged, bulk export produces a ZIP with a hash manifest, and potential matches are leads only.

What taking over involves
- Two named administrators from your organization
- Ownership of the code repository, the database and file storage (Supabase), and the website hosting (Vercel)
- Responsibility for the personal data collected, including a data protection officer and a privacy notice
- Roughly one hour of setup with me. The steps are in the handover guide

What I will do
- Transfer the three accounts, rotate every secret key, and remove my access
- Walk your administrators through the reviewer guide and user management page

What I need from you
- The names of two administrators and an official email address for each
- Confirmation from an officer of the organization through your official page or contact number
- Your decision on who pays for the hosting plan

Please do not send any passwords or keys by message. Access is granted only through service invitations.

Thank you.
